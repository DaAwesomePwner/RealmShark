# Characters

**Characters** (sidebar, **Alt+4**) has the customizable tabs **Roster**, **Exalts** and **Pets**. With saved history open, Analyst mode adds a fourth tab after Pets, **Fame history** ([below](#fame-history-analyst)).

The **Characters → Roster** tab remembers your own characters as capture sees them. Start capture, then enter the game on a character. The existing character-list response, when available from the Pet Yard or Daily Quest Room, also adds characters. Nearby players are not added. Characters missing from a later response are retained.

The roster opens as a **gallery** of character cards: skin sprite, class, level and fame, an 8-pip maxed meter (**—** when unknown, never 0/8), a **Seasonal** chip, when the character last played, and a **Playing now** marker for the character in game. Characters marked dead are grouped in a collapsed **Graveyard** below. **Sort** orders the cards by last played, fame, class or maxed; unknown values sort last. The existing roster table is the **Table view**: in Simple mode choose **Table view** or **Gallery view** in the ⋯ menu; in Analyst mode use the Gallery/Table switch in the filter row, where cards also show character IDs. Both views show exactly the characters the one search and filter drawer select, and the view and sort are remembered. **Save view state** and **Reset saved view state** are in the ⋯ menu; the page warns only when saving fails.

The roster supports literal-text search (class, account, character ID, item name/ID, or notes), task filters and sortable table columns. Equipment search accepts decimal and hexadecimal IDs as well as names. Open a character (Enter or double-click on a card or table row, or click the Home hero) for its full-page **character sheet**; **‹ Characters** or Back returns to the list where you were. The sheet's tabs can be reordered and hidden:

- **Overview**: base stats against class caps (with the live boost while you play), what each stat still needs (with vault potions and how long ago they were counted, when known), equipped gear, a class exalt summary and a **Pet** card (name, rarity and ability levels; open it for the Pet tab).
- **Gear**: equipped items, inventory and backpack; unknown and empty slots stay distinct.
- **Exalts**: this class's eight stats with tier, completions, the distance to the next tier and where to earn it.
- **Pet**: this character's pet as capture last read it: name, rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary or Divine), family, maximum ability level, each ability's level toward that maximum, and when and from where it was observed. The family comes only from the selected game assets' pet names (`pets.xml`); it shows **—** when they do not name the pet. A slot the maximum level has not unlocked yet reads **Locked**. The collapsed **Feeding estimate** drawer estimates items and fame to each ability's next level and to its maximum for the feed power you enter (500 by default); the numbers are estimates (≈) from local formulas and change nothing in game. **No pet** means the character list reported no pet equipped; **Pet not captured yet** means capture has not read this character's pet, which arrives when capture reads your character list in the Pet Yard or the Daily Quest Room.
- **Fame**: this character's fame (live while it is in game, otherwise the saved value and its age), fame per hour for the newest session with at least 10 minutes of readings (only increases count), the fame gained across saved sessions, and a chart of each session's readings. The history is exact per account: a reading belongs to this character only when both its account and its character ID match, because character IDs repeat across accounts. Readings recorded before this version (or before capture saw the account) have no account, so they are excluded and counted ("N older readings have no recorded account and are not shown"), as are saved sessions that cannot be read. Readings are estimated from captured experience, so the rate, the recorded gain and the chart are marked ≈; the Fame tile's live and saved values are the game's own.
- **Build**: weapon damage and recovery estimates for the character you are playing, or after capture stops the last one you played. It replaced the My Info page, and there is no separate Build page. Other characters' sheets point to it. **Alt+7**, Settings search ("Build (weapon damage and recovery)") and the Home hero's Build action open it for the character in game, else the most recently played one. With no character yet they open the Characters list, which says "No characters yet". Back returns to where you were.
- **Goals**: one card per goal of this character, from this character's own account: its stat goals and its class's exalt goals, each with progress toward the fixed target, what remains, its state and, for an exalt, where to earn it. **Reconfirm target** marks a goal whose class caps or dungeon mapping changed since it was set, or whose target is above the cap. The account-wide goals panel (account picker, goal table and editors) stays below the cards: expanded in Analyst mode, and in a collapsed **Manage goals** section in Simple mode, so goals stay editable in both.
- **Notes**, **Death annotation** for a character marked dead, and in Analyst mode **Snapshot evidence** (field source and receipt times, also shown under the header).

Combine account, class, manual life state and season with **Needs Life**, stat coverage, maxed-count range and snapshot age. For example, select an account, **Needs Life** and **All base stats captured** to find known deficits; use **Life need unknown** or **Missing cap definitions** to find records that need better evidence. **Maxed** and **Potions remaining** sort numerically. A total needs all eight base stats and cap definitions: unknown is not zero.

Age filters refer to **Last snapshot update**, not the last time every field was observed or proof that the character is alive. **Reset filters** clears display predicates without deleting records. Filters, sorting, columns and character selection are remembered; notes remain attached to the exact account/character. These filters cover the retained character journal, independently of app-session history pickers.

**Mark dead** preserves the character's last snapshot. **Restore alive** reverses the mark. Death is a manual annotation; disappearing from capture or losing a connection never marks a character dead. If the character is reported again, **Observed again—restore?** offers explicit restoration before accepting updates. Notes survive restoration. Pending new snapshot data is memory-only: after restarting, restore the character and capture it again to refresh its values. Exalts belong to the account/class and survive character death.

The **Characters → Exalts** tab shows one account's saved exalt progress as a grid of class tiles: the class, its total completions, its lowest tier as five pips, an eight-cell strip of each stat's tier and the class's own loot boost. A class's loot boost is 5% per tier of the lowest of the eight counts of every class that shares its weapon (25% once all of them are at 75 or more), or 35% on every class of a fully exalted account (19 or more classes with every count at 75). It shows **—** when the selected game assets do not name the classes that share the weapon; if any class of the group has no saved counts, the boost is 0%, as in game. The header shows the loot boost of the class you are playing on this account, otherwise of the class you last played, and names that class and which of the two it is; beside it, how many observed classes are fully exalted. The grid follows the current account (the one in game, else the last one seen while RealmShark ran, else the account of the most recently played character) when it has saved exalt counts, else the first account that does. When more than one account has saved exalt counts, an **Account** selector appears; the choice is not remembered. Open a tile (Enter, Space or double-click) for that class's detail, the same view as the sheet's Exalts tab; **‹ Exalts** or Escape returns to the grid with that tile still selected. See [Pets and feeding estimates](#pets-and-feeding-estimates) for the Pets tab.

The final cleanup retires five legacy panels that were no longer constructed by the application. The mounted Roster/Exalts/Pets views and live completion, vault, equipped-pet, fame, and journal tracking remain. This does not add replacements for the old unmounted collection grid, completion matrix, aggregate exalt rows, quickslot display, or multi-character/vault potion planner. See the [retirement disposition](STEP-4-CLEANUP.md#legacy-character-retirement).

At compact sizes or enlarged fonts the gallery wraps to fewer cards per row and the page scrolls; the table view keeps usable data rows, and sheet tabs wrap instead of hiding part of a label. Keyboard focus reveals controls through nested scroll panes. Draft notes survive background refreshes and are saved to their character when you leave the sheet or another character's sheet opens. Fame uses the display locale, while IDs stay ungrouped and dates use the shared full timestamp format.

## Data and accuracy

Records save automatically to `Characters/journal.json` in the application's working directory, normally within two seconds and on normal shutdown. Copy this folder to back up or move the roster. Account identities are hashed to prevent character-ID collisions between accounts; display names remain local. Tokens, credentials and raw packets are not written to the journal. The folder is excluded from Git and portable release staging.

Missing stats and equipment remain unknown; an observed empty equipment slot is different from an unobserved slot. Capture needs an account ID and character ID before creating a record. Unknown base stats or missing class assets prevent an exact `x/8` claim. Base stats use the protocol's total minus boost values; caps come from the local extracted `players.xml`. Potion estimates clamp at zero and use +5 Life/Mana and +1 for the other stats. Below level 20, these are deficits against the final class cap; level-up gains can reduce them. Exalt thresholds use the application's existing 5/15/30/50/75 progression. These are snapshots, not a complete historical event log.

**Last observed alive**, **Roster received** and **Marked dead manually** describe different evidence. Snapshot age and known-field counts do not mean every field was refreshed together: omitted fields retain earlier values, and field details identify older observations. Times are local receipt times. Older journals keep their values with **Legacy / provenance unknown** rather than invented timestamps; absent seasonal metadata stays unknown.

Writes replace the journal through a temporary file. If saving fails, the status line shows the failure and the application retries. If an existing journal is corrupt or has an unsupported version, it is preserved and saving is disabled with a visible explanation. Preview mode reads saved records but does not start capture, make requests, or save roster edits.

The journal is saved as **version 5** (pet, dungeon completions, experience, backpack, per-class exalt times and vault potions). The first save of an older file keeps a one-time copy beside it as `journal.v4.bak` before writing version 5, so a rollback is never left without the pre-upgrade data. Older RealmShark builds open the upgraded journal **read-only**: they cannot save new capture over version 5 fields they do not understand. To roll back manually, copy `journal.v4.bak` over `journal.json`; anything captured since the upgrade will not be in it. If the one-time copy cannot be written, nothing is saved: the status line names `journal.v4.bak` and says `journal.json` is unchanged. Move or delete whatever is at `journal.v4.bak`, or free disk space; saving retries automatically.

The sheet's **Fame** tab reads saved session history (see [App sessions and durable history](SESSION-HISTORY.md)). Fame readings saved there now carry the same hashed account key as the journal, never a raw account ID.

## Fame history (Analyst)

**Characters → Fame history** is the saved character fame view that the retired Statistics page offered ([where Statistics went](STATISTICS.md)). It is an Analyst tab, after Pets, and exists only while saved history is open. Simple mode hides it. Settings search ("Character fame history") opens it in Analyst mode; in Simple mode the same entry opens Characters.

- The tab is built the first time you select it and reads saved history only; there is no live view. It opens on every saved session.
- A header line reads "Character fame per saved session: first and last reading, gain and observed time." Beside it, **Open fame session file…** opens a saved `.fame` file read-only in the fame session viewer.
- One row per saved session and character: the first and last fame reading, the change between them and the observed time. Search covers the session, character ID and class; date bounds select fame readings. Undated readings are counted separately and never ordered as if they happened at epoch zero, and incomplete readings get no gain or elapsed time. The Filters drawer's loot facets and dungeon choice do not filter fame, because fame readings do not record a map. Character IDs are scoped by session, and this view does not use the account key yet (the sheet's Fame tab does).
- **Open selected session's full fame graph** opens the selected row's whole session in the fame session viewer, not just the filtered dates.
- The view has its own saved state (`ux.archive.character-fame`), search, named views and exports, as described in [Session history](SESSION-HISTORY.md).

The fame session viewer (a saved session's full graph, or a `.fame` file) lists every character with saved fame or map records, including characters with zero or negative gain, map records only, or a single reading. Tables sort numbers numerically, and an em dash marks a missing fame reading. The character selector drives the graph and the map visits. **With fame gain** only filters the map visits, beside the dungeon selector; it does not remove characters or change the graph's readings. Switching to a character without fame readings clears the graph. Session Info separates the unfiltered saved totals from the counts in the current view. Viewing and filtering never rewrite the saved file. `.fame` files are no longer written ([where Statistics went](STATISTICS.md#fame-sessions)).

## Pets and feeding estimates

**Characters → Pets** is a gallery of the current account's pets: the account in game, else the last one seen while RealmShark ran, else the account of the most recently played character. It always shows the pets your characters have equipped, as the journal last saved them: one card per pet, listing every character that carried it, with **(dead)** for a character marked dead. While you are in the Pet Yard with capture on, it also shows every pet in the yard, marked **In the Pet Yard now**; pets that no character equips are not remembered after you leave the yard. Each card shows the pet's name, rarity, family and its three abilities' levels toward the maximum level (**Locked** for a slot not unlocked yet, **—** when a value was not captured). The line above the cards names the account, whether capture is running, and how many pets are in the yard and equipped. **Explicitly absent** is different from **Not captured**; incomplete pet metadata does not establish that no pet is equipped.

The **Feeding calculator** is in a collapsed drawer below the cards. It estimates for the selected card's pet, or the first card's when none is selected; Enter, Space or a double-click on a card opens the drawer. Its evidence lines name each value's source and receipt time.

- Search local equipment, or enter a numeric/hex item ID and choose **Use item ID** (including food). You can always enter a positive **Feed power per item** manually and choose **Recalculate feeding costs**.
- Each ability explains captured level, points and maximum, the feed-power scenario and ability multiplier, with item/fame estimates for the next level and maximum. Missing inputs, locked abilities and inconsistent inputs are labeled separately. Missing points never mean **Fully fed**; unsupported fame-cost tiers can leave fame unavailable while item counts remain calculable.
- Estimates use local formulas. Choosing an item or feed-power scenario does not establish ownership, feed a pet or consume items. Scroll the page for long explanations and scenario controls in short windows.

## Validation

`scripts/character-validation.gradle` builds in a separate directory, so tests and packaging do not replace the active application JAR. Character tests cover persistence, partial stats, account separation, death/restore, missing roster entries, equipment changes, exalt updates, potion calculations, corrupt files, failed saves, and search/filter/sort controls. UI tests render desktop and compact screenshots. Full live gameplay capture remains a separate check.
